You can reach the AXTO editorial team by email:

**Email:** [hello@axto.us](mailto:hello@axto.us)

We read every message and usually reply within a few working days.

## What to write to us about

- **Corrections.** If you spot an error, an outdated statistic or a broken link, please tell us which guide and, if you can, point us to a source. We correct mistakes promptly and update the review date on the guide.
- **Questions about a guide.** If something in a guide is unclear, let us know. Your question may become a new section that helps other readers too.
- **Topic suggestions.** Tell us what you would like us to cover next: a reading stage, a classroom routine, a type of book or a question your family keeps coming back to.
- **Educators.** Teachers, librarians and reading specialists are welcome to share classroom experience or suggest improvements to our classroom guides.

## What we cannot do

- We cannot assess individual children or give personalized educational, medical or psychological advice. Please speak with your child's teacher, pediatrician or a qualified specialist.
- We do not accept payment for coverage, reviews or links.
- We do not publish guest posts written for marketing purposes.

## Your privacy

We use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not share them with third parties or add you to any mailing list. See our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) for details.
