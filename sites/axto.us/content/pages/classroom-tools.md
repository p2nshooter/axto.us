AXTO — Adventures eXplore TOgether — helps families and teachers raise confident readers. The same publisher also runs two projects outside the world of picture books and phonics, and this page is where we introduce them. Because they are ours, this is an advertisement, and we would rather you heard that from us in the first paragraph.

## axto.io — an AI workspace that is careful with children's information

[axto.io](https://axto.io/?utm_source=axto.us&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) brings several artificial-intelligence tools together under one account. Teachers, reading specialists and homeschooling parents tend to reach for three of them:

- **Prompt Library and AI Studio.** Hundreds of tested prompt templates for different professions, which you can adapt once and save — for example to turn your own notes on a chapter book into discussion questions, or to draft a newsletter for parents. In AI Studio you can compare the answers of several AI models side by side before you use any of them.
- **Privacy Vault.** Before a document reaches an AI, the vault finds names, addresses, dates of birth and other personal details and blacks them out on your own device. When the paper in front of you is a class list or a reading assessment, that matters.
- **Clipper.** A long read-aloud or book-club recording becomes short, captioned clips, cut on your own device, so families can watch the highlights without the raw footage ever being uploaded.

You use your own AI key and axto.io only relays the request; in Local Mode the AI runs entirely on your computer. A rule we hold to on this site applies here too: an AI can help you prepare, but it cannot listen to a child read. Screening, assessment and support plans belong with teachers, reading specialists and the child's family.

## XAA — websites for schools, tutors, libraries and authors

[XAA](https://xaa.es/?utm_source=axto.us&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ecosystem) is a European web development studio that builds websites and platforms to order. In our corner of the world that could be:

1. a tutoring center's website with programs, schedules and online booking;
2. a school or library site that makes events, book lists and volunteer sign-ups easy to find;
3. an online shop or membership platform for a children's author, a reading program or a small publisher.

Work is divided into milestones that are agreed, delivered and paid for one at a time — by PayPal, bank transfer or USDT — so nobody pays for work they have not seen. Ready-made templates help a small budget get started quickly. XAA's own site is available in English, Spanish and Indonesian.

## What stays the same here

AXTO's guides to early literacy, learning to read, choosing books and helping struggling readers are written independently. None of them recommends axto.io or XAA, and none was written or changed because of them. If these tools free up an evening, spend it the best way we know: reading together.
