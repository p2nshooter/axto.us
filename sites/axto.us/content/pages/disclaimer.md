AXTO publishes general information about reading, literacy and child development for parents, caregivers and educators. We work hard to make it accurate and up to date, but please keep its limits in mind.

## Not individual advice

Our guides describe what research and experienced educators suggest for children in general. Every child is different. Nothing on this site is a diagnosis or a personalized recommendation, and it should not replace an assessment by your child's teacher, pediatrician, audiologist, eye care professional, speech-language pathologist, educational psychologist or another qualified specialist.

**If you are concerned about your child's speech, hearing, vision, attention, behavior or reading progress, talk to a professional.** Early support makes a real difference, and waiting to see is not always the best approach.

## Research changes

Education research continues to develop, and professional guidance is updated from time to time. We show the date each guide was last reviewed, but we cannot guarantee every detail reflects the very latest findings at the moment you read it.

## Ages and milestones

When we give ages or grade levels for reading skills, they are typical ranges, not deadlines. Many children reach milestones earlier or later and are developing normally.
