**AXTO** stands for **Adventures eXplore TOgether**. We are an independent publication about one of the most important things a family or a classroom can do together: read.

Reading is the skill underneath almost everything else children learn at school, and it begins long before school does, in the songs, conversations and picture books of the first years. Yet the advice parents and teachers receive is often contradictory. One source says to drill sight words, another says never to. One says audiobooks are cheating, another says they are a lifeline. Reading levels, phonics programs, decodable books, dyslexia screening, screen time: the vocabulary alone can feel like a test.

AXTO exists to make that landscape clear. Each guide explains what children are actually learning at each stage, what the research suggests helps, what it does not support, and what that looks like on an ordinary weeknight at home or a Tuesday morning in a classroom.

## What you will find here

Our guides are organized into six shelves:

- **Early Literacy (0–4):** reading, talking and singing with babies and toddlers.
- **Learning to Read:** phonics, letter sounds, decodable books, fluency and comprehension.
- **Reading at Home:** routines, home libraries, read-alouds, audiobooks and screens.
- **Choosing Books:** picture books, chapter books, graphic novels, nonfiction and reading levels.
- **Struggling Readers:** warning signs, dyslexia, motivation and getting support from school.
- **Classroom & School:** read-alouds, classroom libraries, independent reading and book clubs.

## How we work

Every guide is written and edited by our team using published research, guidance from professional bodies such as the American Academy of Pediatrics and the International Dyslexia Association, and the long practical experience of teachers and librarians. When the evidence is strong, we say so; when it is mixed or still developing, we say that too. Our full approach is described in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).

We review guides regularly and show the date of the latest update on each one.

## Independence

AXTO does not sell reading programs, tutoring or books, and no publisher pays to appear in our guides. When we mention a type of book, a program or a resource, it is because it is useful to our readers, not because of a commercial relationship. The site is free to read and supported by advertising served by Google AdSense, which is kept clearly separate from our editorial content.

## What we are not

We are not a substitute for your child's teacher, pediatrician, speech-language pathologist or an educational psychologist. If you are worried about your child's development, speech, hearing, vision or reading progress, please talk to a qualified professional who can assess your child in person.
