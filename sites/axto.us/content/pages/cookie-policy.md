Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. This page explains which cookies may be used when you visit axto.us and how you can control them.

## Cookies set by AXTO

AXTO itself does not set cookies to track you. Our visit statistics are counted without cookies and without identifying individual readers.

## Advertising cookies from Google

The site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may set cookies, or read existing ones, to:

- show ads and limit how often you see the same ad;
- measure whether ads are displayed and how they perform;
- personalize ads based on your interests, where you have allowed it;
- detect fraud and abuse.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google shows you a consent message before using advertising cookies. If you decline, you can keep reading normally and will see only non-personalized ads.

You can manage personalized ads in [Google's Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) and read [how Google uses cookies in advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Controlling cookies in your browser

Every modern browser lets you see, block and delete cookies:

- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
- **Safari:** Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
- **Edge:** Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

Blocking all cookies will not stop you from reading AXTO, but it may change the ads you see.
