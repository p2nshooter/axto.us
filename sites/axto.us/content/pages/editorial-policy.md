AXTO's purpose is to give parents and teachers clear, trustworthy guidance on reading. This page explains how we make sure our guides deserve that trust.

## Our standards

1. **Useful first.** Every guide answers a real question that families or teachers ask, and ends with something practical you can do.
2. **Evidence-informed.** We base our guidance on published research and on the positions of respected professional bodies. We distinguish between strong evidence, emerging findings and common practice that has not been well studied.
3. **Honest about limits.** If research is mixed, we say so. If an approach is popular but unproven, we say so. If something depends on the individual child, we say that too.
4. **Plain language.** We explain technical terms such as phonemic awareness, decodable text or Lexile measures the first time we use them.
5. **No commercial influence.** No publisher, program or advertiser can buy coverage or influence what we write.

## Sources we rely on

- Peer-reviewed research in education, psychology and linguistics, including major syntheses such as the report of the National Reading Panel.
- Guidance from professional organizations such as the American Academy of Pediatrics, the International Dyslexia Association and the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association.
- Public information from government education agencies, including information about special education under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA).
- The practical experience of classroom teachers, reading specialists and librarians.

When we cite a specific study or report, we describe it accurately, including its size and limits where that matters.

## How a guide is made

Each guide is researched and drafted by a member of our editorial team, then edited for accuracy, clarity and usefulness. We check every factual claim against its source, remove anything we cannot support and make sure the advice is safe and age-appropriate.

## Reviews and updates

We review guides regularly and whenever important new research or guidance appears. The date of the latest review is shown on every guide.

## Corrections

If we make a mistake, we fix it promptly. Significant corrections are reflected in the guide's review date. You can report an error through our [contact page](/contact/).

## Advertising and independence

The site is funded by advertising served by Google AdSense. Ads are clearly separate from our content, and advertisers have no say in our editorial decisions.
