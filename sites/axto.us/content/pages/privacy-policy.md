This privacy policy explains what information is collected when you visit axto.us, how it is used and the choices you have. We aim to collect as little as possible.

## Who we are

axto.us is published by the AXTO editorial team. You can contact us about privacy at [hello@axto.us](mailto:hello@axto.us).

## Information we collect

**Reading without an account.** You can read every guide without registering. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details to use the site.

**Visit statistics.** We record aggregate page views (which page was viewed and when) without setting our own cookies and without identifying individual visitors. We use this only to understand which guides are useful.

**Messages you send.** If you email us, we use your address and message to reply. We keep the correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request.

**Server logs.** Like all websites, our hosting provider processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages securely and protect against abuse. These logs are kept for a short period.

## Advertising: Google AdSense

AXTO shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google's Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can learn more about [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message managed by Google before advertising cookies are used, and can change their choice at any time.

## Children's privacy

AXTO is written for adults: parents, caregivers and educators. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict its processing and to withdraw consent. Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws may request information about the categories of data collected and ask us not to sell or share personal information; AXTO does not sell personal information. To make a request, email [hello@axto.us](mailto:hello@axto.us).

## Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the review date at the top.
